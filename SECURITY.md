# Reporting a security or privacy issue

Please report suspected vulnerabilities or data-protection problems privately to the maintainer (Eduardo Dias) and
do not open a public issue. **Never include real logs, screenshots of real logs, patient identifiers or other PHI in a
report**: describe the problem with synthetic data.

The security design, OWASP ASVS mapping and known limitations are documented in [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).
